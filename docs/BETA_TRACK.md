> **Prepared by:** Agent 6 — April 2026

---

## 1. Track Architecture

Google Play offers three testing tracks before production:

| Track | Visibility | Max Testers | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Internal testing** | Invite only | 100 | Instant approval, no review. Use for dev team sanity checks. |
| **Closed testing (Beta)** | Invite only | Unlimited | Requires Google Play review. Use for controlled beta. |
| **Open testing** | Public opt-in | Unlimited | Public-facing, higher scrutiny. |
| **Production** | All users | Unlimited | Requires 12-tester / 14-day gate if personal account. |

**Recommended path:** Internal → Closed Beta → Production

---

## 2. The "12 Testers for 14 Days" Policy

> **Applies to:** Personal developer accounts created **after November 13, 2023**.

If the CVScan developer account is a personal account created after November 13, 2023, Google Play requires that you complete **at least 14 consecutive days of closed testing** with a minimum of **12 opted-in testers** before you can apply for production access.

### Verification Checklist

- [ ] Check account type: Play Console → Account Details → Account type
  - If **"Personal account"** created after Nov 13, 2023 → 12/14 policy applies
  - If **"Organization account"** or older personal account → policy may not apply (verify in console)
- [ ] Confirm Play Console shows the "Production access" eligibility status in: Release → Production → Countries/regions
- [ ] Note: The 14-day clock starts when testers **opt in** (accept the tester invite), not when you publish the release

## 4. Tester Onboarding Instructions

Send this to all testers via the Play Console invite email + a follow-up message:

---

### Email: CVScan Closed Beta Invite

**Subject:** You're invited to beta test CVScan — AI Resume Coach 🎉

---

Hi [Name],

Thanks for agreeing to help beta test **CVScan**, an AI-powered resume tailor and career coach app!

**Here's how to join:**

1. **Accept the invite:** Click the link below to opt in as a tester.
   *(Link generated by Play Console — insert here)*
   
2. **Install the app:** Once you've opted in, visit the Play Store listing and tap **Install**. The beta version will appear automatically.

3. **Create your account:**
   - Open CVScan and tap **Get Started**
   - Sign in with your Google account or email
   - Complete the short onboarding flow (takes ~2 minutes)

4. **Build your Career Memory:**
   - Go to **Profile** and add at least 1 work experience and your education
   - This unlocks the generation features

5. **Try the core features:**
   - Paste a job description into **Job Fit** or **Tailor** and see your resume score
   - Generate a cover letter via **Copilot**
   - Try the **Scanner** to scan a physical resume (optional)

**Giving feedback:**

Please report any bugs or issues to: **beta@cvscan.com**

Include:
- Your device model and Android version
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- A screenshot if possible

**Important notes:**
- This is a **pre-release build** — some features may be incomplete or behave unexpectedly
- Your data is private and isolated — no other user can see your account
- You can delete your account at any time: **Profile → Delete Account**

Thank you for helping us build a better product! 🙏

— The CVScan Team

### Tester FAQ

**Q: Do I need to pay for anything?**  
A: No. Beta accounts receive free starter credits. You won't be charged unless you purchase additional credits, which is entirely optional.

**Q: Can I share the app with someone else?**  
A: Please don't — this is a closed beta. Additional testers must be separately invited.

**Q: How long is the beta?**  
A: At least 14 days. We'll let you know when the public launch is ready.

**Q: What happens to my data after the beta?**  
A: Your data will be preserved into the production release. You can also delete it at any time.

## 5. Beta Testing Checklist (Per Build Publication)

Before publishing each closed beta build:

- [ ] Internal build passes smoke test on at least 2 physical Android devices
- [ ] Version code incremented in `versionCode` (app/build.gradle or equivalent)
- [ ] Version name matches the release (e.g., `0.1.0-beta.1`)
- [ ] Release notes updated in Play Console (keep testers informed of changes)
- [ ] No test credentials or debug logging included in the build
- [ ] `proguard-rules.pro` / R8 minification reviewed for sensitive class exposure
- [ ] AAB uploaded (not APK) to closed testing track
- [ ] Rollout set to 100% for closed beta testers
- [ ] Tester opt-in count confirmed (≥ 12 before 14-day clock matters)
