# Product Requirements Document

## Product

CVScan is an AI-powered career coaching platform that helps job seekers score resumes, tailor application materials, prepare for interviews, and manage applications in one workflow.

## Problem

Job seekers currently jump between disconnected tools for resume scanning, keyword optimization, job discovery, cover letter writing, interview practice, and application tracking. That creates friction, context loss, and inconsistent application quality.

## Users

- Active job seekers who want to improve resume quality and ATS performance
- Professionals applying across multiple roles who need repeatable workflows
- Users who want guided AI coaching rather than one-off generation

## Value Proposition

CVScan combines resume analysis, job-fit intelligence, tailored generation, interview preparation, and tracking into one guided command center.

## Product Goals

- Help users improve resume-job match quality
- Reduce time required to tailor each application
- Increase confidence through coaching and interview prep
- Build a credit-based SaaS product with clear monetization

## Core User Journey

1. Discover a job or paste a target role
2. Scan resume and identify gaps
3. Tailor bullets, resume sections, and cover letter
4. Save and track the application
5. Prepare for interviews with AI assistance

## MVP Feature Set

- ATS scan and resume scoring
- Tailored bullet and cover letter generation
- Job discovery and company research
- Interview prep and practice
- Application tracking dashboard
- Credit-based billing and usage management

## Non-Goals For Early Releases

- Enterprise recruiting workflows
- Multi-tenant admin tooling
- Deep CRM-grade sales automation
- Complex team collaboration features

## Success Signals

- Users complete end-to-end application workflows in one product
- Core scan and tailoring flows are reliable and fast
- Users return for interview prep and tracking, not just one-time generation
- Paid credit purchases convert after free usage
